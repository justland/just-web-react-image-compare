First release. `ImageCompare` compares two images the way GitHub's image diff does, with a switcher between three modes: side by side, swipe, and onion skin. `SideBySide`, `Swipe`, and `OnionSkin` are also exported on their own.

The components use inline styles and native range inputs, with `react` and `react-dom` as peer dependencies, so they need no CSS file and render inside the Storybook manager.
